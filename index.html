FAQ

FAQ

How far ahead should I book?

As soon as you have a date. Weekends and the end of the month fill up first. Moving sooner? Message us and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Do you bring boxes and packing supplies?

Yes. We bring sturdy boxes, bubble wrap and moving blankets so your things are protected on the way.

Do you do international moves?

Yes, in and out of the US, by sea or air, door to door. Tell us the origin, the destination and roughly what's going and we'll walk you through each step, customs included.

Do you move pianos?

Yes, uprights and grands. Tell us the type, the floor and any stairs and we'll plan the right crew and equipment.

Can you just load or unload my truck?

Yes. If you rented a U-Haul, a POD or a container, we can load or unload it for you.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Windermere and work all over Orlando and Central Florida, plus moves to other states and other countries.
